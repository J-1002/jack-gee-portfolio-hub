Lecture

Local Mac build · preparing for early testing

Problem

Taking notes can compete with listening. Useful material ends up scattered. An exam often covers topics across several lectures.

What I built

A personal study workspace that keeps course context, lecture capture, notes, questions, and exam review in one place. In the current Mac build:

  • Course folders with syllabi, vocabulary, and schedules
  • Structured live notes while listening
  • Ask questions about a saved lecture
  • Continue a lecture across sessions with dated parts
  • Study guides, quizzes, and flashcards from selected material
  • Topic-focused guides across up to 30 lectures, with source references and gaps to check
  • Short two-presenter review podcasts (synthetic Mac voices) plus Markdown export

AI angle

AI helps organize and explore course material — notes, limited transcript corrections, Q&A, and study artifacts — always against the student’s own lectures. Answers still need a critical eye; the value is staying with the source, not replacing judgment.
